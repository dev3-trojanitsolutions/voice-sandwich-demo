# FAQ

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday and Sunday 10am to 6pm.

## Delivery
We deliver anywhere in Bangalore. Delivery costs ₹40 and is free for orders over ₹500.

## Payment
For phone orders we accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or credit card. Orders are confirmed with an OTP sent to your phone number. You can cancel an order any time before it is confirmed. Once the order is placed and confirmed with the OTP, it cannot be cancelled.

## Refunds
If your order is wrong, call us within 1 hour and we will remake it or refund you.
