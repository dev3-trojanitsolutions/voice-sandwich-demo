# Allergens

## Gluten
Butter Naan, the sandwich bread (white and whole wheat), Chilli Chicken and Chicken 65 contain gluten (wheat flour or soy sauce). Biriyani, Mandi, Chicken Kabab and Mutton Korma have no wheat, but they are cooked in the same kitchen, so cross-contact is possible. Ask for gluten-free bread to make any sandwich gluten free.

## Dairy
Butter Naan contains butter and milk. Mutton Korma and the Chicken Kabab marinade contain yoghurt. Biriyani is cooked with ghee and served with raita (yoghurt). Swiss, cheddar and provolone contain dairy.

## Eggs
Mayo contains egg; it is served with Mandi and is a sandwich topping. Chicken 65 and Chilli Chicken batter contain egg. Mustard does not contain egg.

## Nuts
Mutton Korma contains cashew nuts. Biriyani may be garnished with cashews; ask for it without. No dish contains peanuts.

## Soy
Chilli Chicken contains soy sauce.
