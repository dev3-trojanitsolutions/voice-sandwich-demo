# Menu

All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹, INR) and include GST.

## Biriyani
Chicken Biriyani: fragrant basmati rice slow-cooked with spiced chicken, served with raita and pickle, ₹250.
Mutton Biriyani: basmati rice dum-cooked with tender mutton pieces, served with raita and pickle, ₹350.

## Mandi
Chicken Mandi: Arabian-style smoked rice with a quarter roasted chicken, served with mayo and spicy chutney, ₹320.
Mutton Mandi: Arabian-style smoked rice with slow-roasted mutton, served with mayo and spicy chutney, ₹450.

## Starters
Chicken Kabab: chargrilled spiced chicken pieces, 6 pieces, ₹220.
Chilli Chicken: Indo-Chinese fried chicken tossed with green chillies, capsicum and onion, dry or gravy, ₹230.
Chicken 65: spicy deep-fried chicken bites with curry leaves, ₹210.

## Curries
Mutton Korma (khorma): mutton cooked in a rich, mild gravy of yoghurt, cashew and spices, ₹320.

## Breads
Butter Naan: soft tandoor-baked bread brushed with butter, ₹50 each.

## Sandwiches
Turkey sandwich: sliced roast turkey breast, ₹180.
Ham sandwich: honey-glazed ham, ₹180.
Roast beef sandwich: slow-roasted beef, ₹220.
All sandwiches come on white or whole wheat bread. Gluten-free bread is available for ₹40 extra.

## Cheeses
Swiss, cheddar and provolone, for sandwiches. Each cheese adds ₹30.

## Toppings
Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, mayo and mustard, for sandwiches. All toppings are free.
